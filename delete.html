Delete your account and data

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Delete from inside the app Deleted immediately Free, no questions asked

The short version

You can delete your Tailors Estimy shop account yourself, at any time, from inside the app: ☰ Menu → Settings → Delete shop permanently. Every receipt, customer entry, photo, worker, price and setting is deleted from our servers straight away, together with your sign-in.

If you can no longer open the app, email us instead — see section 2. If you only want to remove some data and keep your account, see section 4.

1. Delete your account in the app

You need to be signed in to the shop you want to delete. The app walks you through a few checks first so a shop cannot be deleted by accident or by someone else holding your phone.

  1. Open Tailors Estimy and sign in to your shop.
  2. On the dashboard, tap the ☰ Menu button at the top right, then tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom card, Delete Shop Permanently, and tap Delete shop permanently.
  4. Verify your Gmail — type the email address this shop is signed in with and tap Verify Gmail.
  5. Verify your password — if you signed up with email and password, type your password and tap Verify password. If you sign in with Google, this step is skipped.
  6. Read before you delete — read what will be removed and tick all four boxes to confirm you understand.
  7. Tick I'm not a robot.
  8. Tap Delete shop permanently, then confirm with Delete forever.

When it finishes, the app shows "The shop was deleted permanently." and returns to the sign-in screen. You are signed out on this phone and on every other phone that used the shop.

This cannot be undone. There is no trash folder and no backup we can restore from. If you need any receipt later, share or save its PDF before you delete the shop.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Your data stays on the server, ready for when you sign in again, until you delete it as described here.

2. Can't open the app? Ask us by email

If you have lost your phone, cannot sign in, or have already uninstalled the app, you can ask us to delete the account for you:

  1. Write to [[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]] with the subject "Tailors Estimy account deletion".
  2. Send it from the email address the shop is registered with, and include the shop name, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account and all its data, exactly as in section 3, and reply to confirm.

Email requests are handled within 30 days, usually much sooner. If we cannot confirm that the request comes from the account owner, we will reply and ask for more detail rather than delete someone else's shop.

3. What is deleted, and what is kept

3.1 Deleted permanently and immediately

DataWhere it was stored
Every receipt: customer names and phone numbers, measurements, garments, notes, blackboard sketches, delivery dates, amounts, GST, advance and payment statusCloud Firestore
Every reference photo attached to a receiptCloud Storage
Your workers and which receipts were handed to themCloud Firestore
Shop details: shop name, owner name, address, mobile number, UPI ID, GST rate, taglines, product rates, language and the receipt number counterCloud Firestore
The list of phones signed in to the shopCloud Firestore
Your sign-in: email address, name and password (or Google sign-in link)Firebase Authentication

After deletion, registering again with the same email address starts a brand-new, empty shop numbered from #00001.

3.2 What is not deleted, and why

  • Copies you already sent. Receipt PDFs and WhatsApp messages you shared with customers or workers are on their phones and in those apps. We cannot reach them.
  • Advertising data held by Google. Ads in the app are served by Google AdMob, which keeps its own data under Google's retention schedules, not ours. You can reset or delete your advertising ID on your phone in Settings → Google → Ads, and manage ad data at My Ad Center.
  • Records the law requires. If we receive an email deletion request, we may keep that email exchange as proof the request was carried out. We do not keep any of your shop or customer data.

Google, which hosts the service, may take a short time to clear deleted data from its internal systems and backups. That data is not accessible to the app, to us, or to anyone else during that time.

4. Delete only some data, without deleting your account

You can remove individual pieces of data at any time and keep using the app:

To deleteDo this in the app
One receipt and everything on it — the customer's name, phone, measurements, notes, sketch and photosOpen the receipt from the dashboard, tap the red bin icon (Delete receipt) at the top right, then Delete.
One reference photo on a receiptOpen the receipt, tap Edit, remove the photo, then save the receipt.
A workerOpen Workers from the dashboard, tap the ⋮ button on the worker, then Delete worker.
All your product rates☰ Menu → Set Product Rate → Reset Product Rate.
Shop details such as address, mobile number, UPI ID or taglines☰ Menu → Settings, clear or change the field, then Save changes.

Each of these deletions is permanent and takes effect immediately.

Customers of a shop who want their own details removed should ask that shop. The shop can delete the customer's receipts in seconds as shown above.
